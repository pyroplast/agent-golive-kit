## Components
Bob Companion answers natural-language questions, surfaces insights and triggers actions. Contextual agents work across hiring, performance, learning, skills and payroll. MCP connects Bob to ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot and Slack with live people context. Bob Studio, for building custom AI experiences, is listed as coming soon.

## Data handling
Zero data retention policy. HiBob and its AI providers do not use customer data to train models. Each customer's AI activity is isolated. AI respects the same permissions as Bob and cannot surface data the user is not allowed to see. Admins can turn AI features on or off at any time in AI Settings.

## Human oversight
Write actions require explicit user confirmation and follow permission rules. Bob AI is not an automated decision-making tool; people make final decisions on employees and compensation.

## Not published
Pricing for AI, accuracy metrics, which model providers are used, and a release date for Bob Studio are not stated.
