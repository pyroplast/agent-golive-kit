## Scope
Data & Analytics is part of Core. It is not a standalone BI tool. Workforce data lives in one governed system: employee records, job architecture, skills mapping, reporting lines. Org charts update automatically as changes happen.

## Reporting
Real-time workforce analytics, role-aware segmentation, leader-specific metrics, configurable dashboards, cross-team reporting and role-based data access. Shared definitions and calculation logic apply across suites.

## Predictive analytics
The FAQ says predictive analytics is supported, but no specific predictive features are described.

## AI analytics
Automated insights that summarize trends into executive and board-ready narratives; InsightsIQ for trend discovery and cost control; a data analyzer that answers natural-language questions about HR data.

## Customer example
Montu grew from 30 to 450 employees on Bob and reduced manual work with automations.
