## Compliance
HMRC-recognised payroll with automated tax code updates, tax code and student loan sync, minimum and living wage compliance alerts, P11D and year-end reporting, continuous legislative updates.

## Worker types
Monthly, weekly and bi-weekly cycles. Supports IR35 contractors, zero-hour, seasonal, part-time and shift workers, and overtime.

Optional managed payroll service at flexible service levels, staffed by CIPP-certified experts.
