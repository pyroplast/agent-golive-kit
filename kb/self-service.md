## Self-service
Included in Core. Employees update profiles and submit change requests. Requests and approvals run through one centralized workflow with configurable approval paths, real-time status and approval history kept for audit.

## Homepage
A homepage shows company announcements, new joiners, milestones, celebrations, peer recognition and quick links to tasks and tools.

## Mobile
Native iOS and Android apps (Bob HR). Employees and managers can complete tasks and approvals on mobile. Mobile access is role-based.

## Localization
Multi-language support with user-level language preferences; local date, time and number formats; regional naming conventions and address fields; localized notifications. The HiBob website itself is available in English, German, Spanish (Spain), Hebrew, Dutch and Swedish. The full list of in-product languages is not published on this page.

## AI in self-service
Product navigator (natural-language navigation), growth coach, email composer, writing assistance for feedback, AI course suggestions, 360 review summaries, goals and key results generator. Sharing AI analysis to Slack is listed as coming soon, not available yet.
