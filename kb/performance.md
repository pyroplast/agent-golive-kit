## Reviews
360 reviews: self, peer, manager and upward. Customizable templates, timelines, approval flows, smart reminders, and a manager companion for drafting reviews. Goals, feedback and prior reviews are pulled into each cycle.

## Calibration
Calibration agent, standalone or integrated calibration events, drag-and-drop 9-box grid, comparison across teams.

## Goals
OKR support, AI-assisted goal drafts, goal levels matching the org structure, goals linked to reviews.

## 1-on-1s and surveys
1-on-1 templates with meeting history. Engagement and lifecycle surveys with anonymous responses, AI theme and sentiment analysis, and heatmaps.

## Succession and development
AI-suggested successors based on skill match, readiness and retention risk tracking, AI-drafted development plans.

## Limits
AI does not evaluate employees. It summarizes, drafts and surfaces themes; managers and HR own performance decisions.

## Packaging
Talent Suite bundles the Talent modules. HiBob's onboarding team configures the platform to the customer's workflows.
