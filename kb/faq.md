## Company
HiBob makes Bob. Founded by CEO Ronni Zehavi and CTO Israel David. UK-registered and headquartered, with offices in several regions.

## Who it is for
Growing companies, split into Growth (under 200 employees), Scale (200-1,000) and Enterprise (1,000+).

## Category
An HCM platform with HRIS foundations, extending into talent, payroll, planning, compensation and analytics.

## Payroll
Native payroll for the US and UK; other countries supported through Payroll Hub.

## Reliability
HiBob claims consistent 99.9% uptime.

## Compliance
Complies with ISO 27001, SOC 1 Type 2, SOC 2 Type 2 and HIPAA, and aligns with GDPR principles.

## Competitors
Positions itself against Rippling, Workday, BambooHR and Personio.

## Cost
Custom pricing based on employee count, modules and implementation or support services. Core HR is in every subscription.

## Implementation time
Mid-market organizations can go live in weeks, depending on size, complexity and modules.
