## Time Off (in Core)
Included in Core. Covers holidays, sick days, PTO and other leave. Policies by location, department or employee group. Shared team calendars. Approved leave syncs to payroll.

## Time & Attendance (add-on)
Sold as an add-on to Core. Clock in/out several times a day or log hours by project, edit entries retroactively, mobile clock-in through the Bob app, real-time manager visibility. Supports hourly and salaried staff and multiple work patterns.

## Payroll sync
Tracked hours and approved time off sync automatically to Bob's UK Payroll and US Payroll.

## Reporting
Reports on clock-in/out times, average daily hours, overtime, holiday, sick and vacation data.

## AI agents
Time Off Agent checks availability, submits requests and answers policy questions. Attendance Agent manages timesheets and clock-in/out.
