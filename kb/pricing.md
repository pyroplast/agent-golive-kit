## Pricing model
No public prices or named tiers. Pricing is quote-based, customized by company size, needs and chosen modules, and generally calculated per employee. Pricing may vary slightly by region, especially the UK and Europe, but the structure is the same everywhere.

## Core is always included
Every customer starts with Core. Core includes Bob AI Companion, the employee database, document management, Time Off, lifecycle workflows, integrations, analytics and reporting, and the mobile app.

## Add-on modules
Add-ons: Talent, Learning, Hiring, Planning, Compensation, UK Payroll, US Payroll, Payroll Hub, and a Sandbox testing environment. Modules can be added or removed later.

## Quote process
Submit a request, speak with a HiBob team member, receive a tailored quote. The quote form uses company size bands 1-49, 50-199, 200-999 and 1,000+ employees.

## Social proof
5,500+ customer companies. Case studies include Chili Piper, EcoOnline and Ecosio. G2 rating 4.5 from 2,335 reviews; Capterra 4.6 from 177 reviews.
