## What Core is
Core is the HRIS foundation of Bob. It is required and is not an add-on; every other suite (Talent, Payroll, Planning, Finance) runs on Core's shared people data and workflows. Core holds people data, org structure and HR operations in one system.

## Core capabilities
Role-based employee self-service, separate employee and manager experiences, mobile apps, job architecture, skills and role data, real-time dashboards for headcount, DEI and attrition, joiner/mover/leaver workflows, approvals and routing, policy-based rules, audit-ready process history.

## Integrations in Core
Marketplace integrations and APIs, SSO for identity, access and provisioning, attribute-based sync across systems. Named integrations include Zoom, LinkedIn, Slack, Udemy, Go1, Google Calendar, Canditech and Microsoft Outlook.

## Global
Localization and multi-language environments, regional workflows and global governance.

## Sandbox
A Sandbox environment lets customers test processes without affecting the live setup. It is sold as an add-on.
