## How integrations work
Integrations cover payroll, finance, IT and HR systems. They are mainly API-based, with some SFTP integrations depending on the vendor. Most sync in real time or at defined lifecycle events.

## Custom integrations
Custom integrations can be built on the Public HR API, which supports webhooks. If an integration is not listed, a marketplace partner can build it.

## Named partners

Hiring and learning: Greenhouse, Teamtailor, 360Learning. Payroll, benefits and time: PayEm, ADP, DATEV, Workato, Deputy, Multiplier, Papaya Global, Perk, Oyster, Quinyx.
