## Scope
Workflows & automation is part of Core and supports every suite. It automates onboarding, approvals, document flows and policy rules.

## Onboarding
Workflows start automatically from lifecycle events, vary by role and location, assign tasks across teams, and track completion and exceptions.

## Approvals
Structured, multi-step approval routing with defined task ownership and visible status.

## Documents and eSign
Documents generated from live data, built-in eSign workflows, version control, audit trails and secure storage.

## Policies and permissions
Role-based permissions, location-based policy logic, approval rule enforcement.

## Workflow design
No-code configuration with conditional logic and branching, and variations by region and worker type.

## AI skills features
Skills generator creates role-specific skill lists; proficiency levels per role can be defined automatically.
