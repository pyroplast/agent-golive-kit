## Pay bands
Pay ranges by role, level and site, defined in a Job Catalog. Pay bands can be shared with managers and employees.

## Cycles and budgets
Customizable compensation cycle workflows, real-time budget tracking, scenario and what-if modeling, Finance-approved guardrails, alerts before budget overruns.

## Pay equity
Gap analysis by role, level, gender and location; alerts for out-of-band proposals; AI equity audits; audit-ready reports.

## Approvals
Multi-layer routing by role level, budget impact, location and hierarchy, with extra sign-offs for senior executives and full audit trails.

## Global
Multi-currency, multi-entity and multi-geo cycles with local pay band adjustments.

## Implementation
Pre-built templates. Most mid-market organizations go live in weeks. Skroutz says running compensation in Bob saved about a week of work.
