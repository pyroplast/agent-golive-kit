## Payroll Hub
Connects to third-party payroll providers including ADP, Paylocity, TriNet and Xero, among others. Holds payroll data from multiple systems in one hub, syncs it, pushes changes to the provider automatically, and shows a dashboard with payroll readiness and alerts.

## Payroll Connect
A no-code setup wizard to connect payroll systems that have no preconfigured integration. Maps fields for new hires, changes, terminations, time off, file uploads and attendance.

## Pay cycles
Weekly, bi-weekly, monthly and bi-monthly cycles, several at once; unlimited pay cycles can connect to one payroll system.

## Benefits administration
Managed inside Payroll Hub. Connects to benefits providers bswift and Sequoia.

## Native vs global payroll
Bob runs native payroll only in the US and UK. Other countries are handled through Payroll Hub with a third-party provider.

## AI
AI payslip companion answers employees' payslip questions.
