## ATS
Applicant tracking is built into Bob: customizable pipelines and stages, hiring teams, evaluation templates, job posting, candidate self-scheduling, and handoff from hire into onboarding. Covers requisition to onboarding, including headcount planning.

## Sourcing
Access to 2,300+ global job boards, a branded career page and built-in employee referrals.

## Candidate experience
Native Google Calendar and Outlook Calendar integration, automated reminders, a candidate portal, data privacy controls.

## AI in hiring
AI CV matching against role criteria with match scores and supporting evidence, AI scorecard generator, evaluation summaries, AI job descriptions, AI email composer, and a hiring assistant that answers questions on openings, candidates and pipeline health. Bob AI does not make hiring decisions; recruiters and managers do.

## Analytics
Hiring KPI dashboards, source effectiveness, time-to-hire, offer acceptance rate, hiring quality linked to lifecycle analytics.

## Global and implementation
Supports multiple languages and time zones. The vendor team helps configure workflows and integrate data; no implementation timeline is published for Hiring.
